Investment Portfolio Simulator

You have $10,000 to invest. Choose how to divide your money, then simulate market days to see how random gains and losses affect your portfolio.

Classroom simulation: Results are fictional and randomly generated. They are not real market data or predictions. Real investments can behave differently, and diversified portfolios can still lose money.
